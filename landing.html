Trust and safety

Built to keep corpers safe.

Opportunities only help when people can trust them.

  • Optional verificationUpload a photo of your camp-issued NYSC ID card from your profile. Our team reviews it by hand. The image is deleted shortly after the decision and moderators never see it.
  • Easy reportingReport any post, person or listing. Scam reports are reviewed first.
  • Private by defaultYour PPA and exact location are hidden unless you choose otherwise. Phone numbers on listings show only when someone taps reveal.
  • Community moderators and our teamVolunteer corps members moderate their communities. Our staff handle serious cases and appeals.
Never pay to get a job.Any job that asks for a fee is a scam. Inspect a place before you pay for it. A verified badge confirms NYSC membership, not that a listing is genuine.
On your phone

Add it to your home screen.

Corpers Connect is an installable web app. No store download, and it is light on data.

On Android

  1. Open Corpers Connect in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Open it from your home screen like any app.

On iPhone

  1. Open Corpers Connect in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open it from the home screen to get notifications.
Questions

Quick answers.

Is Corpers Connect run by NYSC?

No. It is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Youth Service Corps. Verified members and moderators are corps members, not NYSC officials.

Do I need to be verified to use it?

No. You can use the app right after signing up. You can ask questions, post and join public communities. Verification unlocks posting jobs, items, rooms and events, creating communities and official batch, camp and state communities.

How does verification work?

Upload a clear photo of the NYSC ID card issued at camp, plus your call-up number. Our team reviews it by hand, usually within 48 hours. The image is deleted 7 days after the decision.

Can I get a job through the app?

Corps members share jobs, gigs and internships. You contact the poster on WhatsApp or by phone. Never pay a fee to get a job or an interview. Report any listing that asks for money.

Is there messaging?

Not yet. Contact for jobs, rooms and items happens through WhatsApp or phone, chosen by the person who posted.

What happens when I pass out?

Your account stays. Your status changes to Alumni and you keep your posts, followers and communities.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no payments inside the app.
